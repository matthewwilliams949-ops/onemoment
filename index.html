one moment

A pause before the apps you open without deciding to.

Pick the apps you want to use less. one moment puts a calm screen in front of them — a breath, and the number of times you have opened that app today. Then it lets you through, every time. It never blocks you and it never scolds you.

Nothing leaves your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics.
